# Seven-Minute Evaluation Demo Walkthrough

1. **Setup (0:00 - 1:00)**: Place Phone A and Phone B in **Aeroplane Mode**. Launch iTantra and show zero active cellular or internet connection.
2. **Push-to-Talk (1:00 - 2:30)**: Speak in Hindi on Phone A. Phone B receives the 52-byte encrypted frame over Wi-Fi Direct and speaks Hindi voice.
3. **Cross-Language Alert (2:30 - 4:00)**: Set Phone B to Tamil. Phone A triggers an Evacuation Alert. Phone B announces the alert in Tamil at forced maximum volume.
4. **Offline Relay (4:00 - 5:30)**: Move Phone C out of range of Phone A. Phone B relays the packet seamlessly.
5. **Diagnostics & Telemetry (5:30 - 7:00)**: Display memory graph showing resident RAM < 250MB and CPU dropping to 0% during idle.
