iTantra — System Design & Architecture Rationale

Core Thesis: "What crosses the link is not audio. It is meaning, and meaning is small."

Why Waveform Compression Fails at 300 bps

Traditional codecs (PCM, AMR, Opus) attempt to preserve and compress the acoustic waveform. At minimum bitrates (~6 kbps for Opus), a 3-second sentence still requires over 2,250 bytes, rendering low-bitrate links completely unusable.

The Neural Transceiver Paradigm

By executing lightweight quantized STT locally on the sender device, iTantra compresses voice into structured text payloads (under 52 bytes encrypted). The receiver re-synthesizes the voice on-device using local VITS models, achieving a 1,600× compression ratio.
